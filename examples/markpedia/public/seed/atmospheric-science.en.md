## Overview

**Atmospheric science** studies the composition, properties and interactions of the atmosphere, from the microphysics of thunderstorms out to the global climate. It links astronomy (solar driving), oceanography (the hydrological cycle) and geoscience (the surface boundary).

## Structure and layers

From the surface upward lie the troposphere, stratosphere, mesosphere, thermosphere and exosphere. The troposphere holds nearly all the water vapour and all weather; its depth varies with latitude and season. The stratosphere is stable because ozone absorbs ultraviolet radiation, and it is the airspace of airliners and weather balloons.

## The energy budget

The shortwave solar radiation the atmosphere absorbs roughly balances the longwave infrared radiation the surface emits, and that balance maintains the temperature structure. Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit infrared, changing how much energy escapes to space — the physical mechanism behind the greenhouse effect.

## Research focus

- Cloud and precipitation microphysics: nucleation, growth and precipitation efficiency of droplets and ice crystals.
- Aerosols and air quality: how fine particulates affect radiation and health.
- Climate change: detecting past change with models and projecting future scenarios.
