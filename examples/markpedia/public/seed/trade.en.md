## Overview

**Trade** is the exchange of goods and services between economies. It lets countries specialise in areas of comparative advantage and widens consumer choice, while also exposing economies to external demand and supply-chain disruptions.

## Comparative advantage and specialisation

Ricardo showed that even if one country is more efficient at everything, trade benefits both sides whenever opportunity costs differ. If producing one unit of good A costs 2 units of good B at home but 3 units abroad, the two economies gain room to specialise and exchange. The first gain from trade is the extra total output specialisation allows; the second is the wider range of goods consumers can choose.

## Scale and structure of trade

World merchandise exports reached about 23 trillion US dollars in 2023, and adding services lifts total trade to more than a fifth of world GDP. The trade balance can be written $ D = X - M $: a positive figure means net exports, a negative one net imports. Taiwan is a classic export-led economy: its 2023 exports of roughly 349 billion US dollars equalled about 46 % of GDP, and electronics components accounted for close to 40 % of the total, making exports unusually sensitive to the semiconductor cycle.

## Tariffs and free trade

A tariff is a tax on imported goods: it raises domestic prices, cuts import volumes and, in the standard analysis, destroys consumer surplus, which is why economists treat it as a costly instrument. The World Trade Organization, founded in 1995 with 166 members, maintains the multilateral trade rules and settles disputes, while Taiwan has signed free trade agreements with mainland China and a range of partners since 2002.

| Instrument | Nature | Main effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tariff | Tax on imported goods | Raises domestic prices and cuts import volumes |
| Import quota | Numerical cap on imports | Replaces tariffs with quantity controls |
| Free trade agreement | Mutual tariff reduction | Lowers trade costs and attracts investment |
| WTO rules | Multilateral trade system | Restricts discrimination and subsidisation |
