## 哲學 — Philosophy

Logic, ethics and schools of philosophy.

Philosophy works mainly through conceptual analysis and argumentative reasoning. Logic studies the form of reasoning itself, ethics addresses how one ought to act, and schools of philosophy organize shared commitments and methods into traditions.
