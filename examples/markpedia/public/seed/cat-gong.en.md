## Field: 工 — Engineering and Technology

Information, mechanical and electrical engineering.

The 工 field turns scientific principles into usable engineering: information technology handles software and networks, mechanical engineering handles mechanisms and materials, electrical engineering handles electricity and signals, and civil engineering handles the structures that carry society.
