## Overview

**Lifestyle and health** examines how sleep, exercise, stress management, smoking and alcohol affect health. Because most of these are modifiable risk factors, they offer the most cost-effective targets in preventing chronic disease.

## Sleep

Adults need roughly $7$ to $9$ hours of sleep a night, and both shorter than $6$ hours and longer than $9$ hours are associated with higher cardiovascular and metabolic risk. Short sleep raises sympathetic activity, cortisol and ghrelin while lowering leptin, so blood pressure climbs and food intake increases. Standard remedies include a fixed bedtime and waking time, avoiding screens for an hour before bed, and cutting caffeine at least $6$ hours before sleep.

## Exercise and sedentary time

The World Health Organization recommends $150$ to $300$ minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week for adults, or $75$ to $150$ minutes of vigorous activity, plus muscle strengthening on at least $2$ days. Regular activity improves insulin sensitivity, blood pressure and mood, and is associated with roughly $20$ % to $30$ % lower all-cause mortality. The harm of sitting is partly independent of exercise volume, so the advice is to break up sitting every $30$ to $60$ minutes.

## Stress, tobacco, alcohol and other habits

Chronic stress acts through cortisol and the sympathetic nervous system to affect blood pressure, sleep and immune function, and consistent associations link it to inflammatory cardiovascular disease as well as anxiety and insomnia. Smoking is the leading avoidable cause of death, with tobacco responsible for around $8$ million deaths worldwide each year, while alcohol is tied to cirrhosis, cancer and road traffic injuries with no clear safe threshold.

| Behaviour | Target | Risk reduced |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sleep | $7$ to $9$ hours a night | Cardiovascular disease, diabetes |
| Aerobic exercise | $150$ to $300$ minutes per week | About $20$ % to $30$ % of all-cause mortality |
| Strength training | Twice a week | Bone density, sarcopenia, glucose control |
| Tobacco | None | Lung cancer, cardiovascular disease |
| Alcohol | As little as possible | Cirrhosis, cancer, accidents |
| Sedentary time | Stand up every $30$ to $60$ minutes | Metabolic syndrome |
