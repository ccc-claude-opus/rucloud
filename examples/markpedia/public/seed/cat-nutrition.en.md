## 營養健康 — Nutrition and health

Nutrients, diet and healthy living.

Nutrition and health concerns what is eaten, how much, and how the rest of life fits in. Nutrients are the necessary chemical components, diet is the daily arrangement, and lifestyle and health adds variables such as sleep and exercise.
