## 生命科技 — Biotechnology

Genetic engineering and biotechnology.

Biotechnology uses biological systems as tools or as subjects. Genetic engineering rewrites genetic information directly, while biotechnology covers the wider range of uses, from fermentation production to diagnostic tools.
