## 機械製造 — Mechanical engineering

Machines, materials and manufacturing processes.

Mechanical engineering turns relations between motion and force into manufacturable devices. The machine defines how it moves, materials set the stress it can bear, and the manufacturing process turns drawings into actual parts.
