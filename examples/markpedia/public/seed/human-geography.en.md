## Overview

**Human geography** studies the spatial distribution and interaction of people, settlements, economic activity and political and cultural phenomena. It links geography to the social sciences, asking where people are and why.

## Fields of study

The main branches are population geography, economic geography, urban geography, political geography and cultural geography, dealing respectively with population and migration, the location of industry, urban structure, borders and geopolitics, and the spatial spread of language and religion. The common method is spatial analysis: treating phenomena as distributed across the surface and shaped by identifiable mechanisms, such as the hexagonal model of service hierarchy derived from central place theory in $1933$.

| Branch | Subject | Core question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Population geography | Distribution, density and migration | Where people go, and why |
| Economic geography | Industry, markets and transport | The economic logic of location |
| Urban geography | Urban form and extent | Where the city ends |
| Political geography | Borders, states and geopolitics | Spatial control and conflict |

## Population and urbanisation

The UN World Population Outlook puts the global population at about $8.2$ billion in mid-2024, and the distribution is extremely uneven. More than half the world's population now lives in cities, and the urban share is projected to approach seven tenths by $2050$. Falling fertility and longer life expectancy drive the demographic transition in most developing countries, while labour migration reshapes the city structure of emerging economies.

## Spatial models and applications

Christaller's central place theory of $1933$ explained the hexagonal hierarchy of service locations, while Weber's industrial location theory weighed transport costs against labour costs. Contemporary work uses GIS for suitability assessment and network analysis, such as choosing logistics sites, delimiting commuter catchments, or estimating how a new infrastructure project reshapes regional development.
