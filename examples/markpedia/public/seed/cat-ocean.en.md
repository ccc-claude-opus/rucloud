## 海洋 — Oceanography

Ocean currents, tides and marine ecosystems.

The ocean covers seventy percent of the surface, stores enormous heat and carbon, and supports a vast food web. Currents and tides describe how the water moves; marine ecology describes how the life in it interacts with each other and with the environment.
