## 數學 — Mathematics

Algebra, geometry and analysis.

Mathematics is a web of definitions and theorems that support each other. Algebra handles symbols and structures, geometry handles shape and space, and analysis studies the limiting behaviour of functions. All three share one syntax: define first, then prove.
