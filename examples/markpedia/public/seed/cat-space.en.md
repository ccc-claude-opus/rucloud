## 太空科技 — Space technology

Rockets, satellites and space probes.

Space technology extends engineering capability beyond the Earth: rockets provide the thrust to leave the surface, satellites keep data orbiting the planet, and probes sample far more distant environments. Their shared difficulty is that mass, fuel and communications all get harder in vacuum and cold.
