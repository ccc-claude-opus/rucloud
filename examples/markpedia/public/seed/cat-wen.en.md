## Field: 文 — Humanities and Languages

Literature, history, philosophy and languages.

The 文 field handles human symbols and thought: literature organizes experience through narrative and poetry, linguistics describes language scientifically, history reconstructs the past, and philosophy interrogates concepts and arguments.

Markpedia organizes articles under the classic nine fields
天 (Astronomy)、地 (Earth)、文 (Humanities)、法 (Law)、商 (Business)、理 (Science)、工 (Engineering)、醫 (Medicine)、農 (Agriculture).
