## Overview

A **typhoon** is an intense tropical cyclone forming over the northwest Pacific, with a very low central pressure, violent winds and heavy rain. About $26$ tropical cyclones are named each year globally, and typhoons are the most intense of them.

## Naming and classification

Names for northwestern Pacific storms are supplied by the member countries of the WMO Typhoon Committee from a list of $140$ names in Chinese, English, French, Spanish and Portuguese, rotated every four years. Intensity is graded by central pressure and maximum sustained wind; the Japan Meteorological Agency calls a system a typhoon when the $10$-minute mean wind reaches $64$ knots, about $118$ km/h.

| Category | Maximum wind | Central pressure | Character |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tropical depression | below $17.2$ m/s | above $1006$ hPa | No closed circulation yet |
| Tropical storm | $17.2$–$24.4$ m/s | $997$–$1006$ hPa | Distinct rotation develops |
| Severe tropical storm | $24.5$–$32.6$ m/s | $985$–$996$ hPa | Winds intensify |
| Typhoon | $32.7$–$41.4$ m/s | $975$–$984$ hPa | Eye forms, heavy rain |
| Severe typhoon | $41.5$–$50.9$ m/s | $950$–$974$ hPa | Marked destructive force |
| Super typhoon | $51.0$ m/s and above | below $950$ hPa | Extreme intensity |

## Formation and structure

Tropical cyclones need sea surface temperatures above $26.5$ °C, ample moisture and enough Coriolis deflection, which is why they form in the northwest Pacific, the North Atlantic and the southern Indian Ocean but not near the equator. A mature cyclone has a calm eye at the centre, an eyewall where the strongest updrafts and winds sit, and spiral rainbands extending outward.

## Energy and damage

A mature typhoon releases latent heat of order $10^{15}$ J per day through condensation, comparable to several hundred tonnes of TNT. Typhoon Haiyan in $2013$ reached about $315$ km/h in sustained wind, devastated the Philippines and killed some $6300$ people. Rainfall often exceeds $500$ mm, triggering landslides and urban flooding, while storm surge does heavy damage along low-lying coasts.
