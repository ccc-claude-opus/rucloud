## 法律 — Law

Statutes, precedent and legal thought.

Law operates through two mechanisms: written rules and decided cases. Statutes supply general norms, precedent grounds principles in concrete disputes, and jurisprudence keeps asking whether either is legitimate and how it should be interpreted.
