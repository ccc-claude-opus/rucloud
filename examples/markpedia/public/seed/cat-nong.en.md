## Field: 農 — Agriculture and Biology

Agriculture, forestry, fisheries and biology.

The 農 field concerns how humans coexist with other living things. Agriculture converts wild resources into food, biology provides the basic understanding of how life works, forestry and fisheries handle the production of different resources, and biotechnology uses living systems themselves as instruments.
