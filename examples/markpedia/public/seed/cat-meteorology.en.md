## 氣象大氣 — Meteorology and atmospheric science

Weather systems, forecasting and atmospheric science.

Meteorology and atmospheric science study the layer of air surrounding us. Weather systems are the moving units behind short-term differences, forecasting turns observations into a probabilistic future, and atmospheric science takes the longer view on composition, energy balance and climate change.
