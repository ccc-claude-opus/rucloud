## 經濟 — Economics

Microeconomics, macroeconomics and trade.

Economics studies how resources are allocated. Microeconomics looks at the choices of individuals and firms, macroeconomics at aggregate output and prices, and trade places exchange between economies in a framework of international division of labor.
