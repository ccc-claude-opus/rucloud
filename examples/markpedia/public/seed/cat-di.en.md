## Field: 地 — Earth and Ocean

The Earth, the oceans and geography.

The 地 field studies the structure of the Earth and the human relations on it: rock and geologic history, ocean motion and ecology, the spatial distribution of landforms, and the causes and defenses of natural hazards.
