## 天文 — Observational astronomy

Telescopes, constellations and how celestial objects are measured.

Astronomy learns about the universe by looking: collect the light of distant objects, measure their brightness, position and spectrum, and infer their nature and evolution from those numbers. This category covers three angles — how we look (telescopes), where we look (constellations) and how precisely we measure (astrometry).
