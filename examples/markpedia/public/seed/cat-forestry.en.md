## 林漁牧 — Forestry, fisheries and animal husbandry

Forestry, fisheries and livestock farming.

Forestry, fisheries and animal husbandry all convert wild resources into products, but on different subjects. Forestry works on trees and forests, fisheries on aquatic resources, and livestock farming on domesticated animals.
