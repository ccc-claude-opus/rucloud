## 自然災害 — Natural hazards

Earthquakes, typhoons, volcanoes and disaster preparedness.

The causes of natural hazards lie in physical mechanisms of the Earth, but how damaging they become depends on exposure and vulnerability. This category covers the causes, scale and preparedness for three classic hazards: earthquakes, typhoons and volcanoes.
