## Field: 理 — Natural Sciences

Mathematics, physics and chemistry.

The 理 field studies the underlying structure of the world. Mathematics supplies a formal language, physics describes matter and spacetime, chemistry studies composition and transformation, and statistics is the discipline of robust inference from imperfect observation.
