## 行星科學 — Planetary science

Solar system planets, asteroids and exoplanets.

Planetary science treats the solar system as a laboratory: eight planets and countless small bodies provide a full sample from rocky to gaseous, letting researchers compare formation mechanisms, interiors and histories. The discovery of exoplanets carries that method to other stellar systems.
