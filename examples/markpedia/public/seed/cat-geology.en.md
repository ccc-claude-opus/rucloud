## 地質 — Geology

Rocks, tectonic plates and the history of the Earth.

Geology reconstructs the history of the Earth from rocks and strata. Rocks preserve the environment of their formation, plate tectonics supplies the engine behind earthquakes, mountains and shifting continents, and geologic history arranges all of this evidence into a four-billion-year sequence.
