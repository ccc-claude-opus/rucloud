## Field: 醫 — Medicine and Health

Medicine, public health and nutrition.

The 醫 field concerns the human body and the maintenance of health. Medicine handles diagnosis and treatment for individual patients, public health handles population-level disease control and cost sharing, pharmacy handles the medicines themselves, and nutrition handles everyday diet and lifestyle.
